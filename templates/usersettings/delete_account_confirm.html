Delete your account

This permanently deletes your Interlink Foundry account. It can't be undone. {% if role_profile %}Just want to step away? You can archive your {{ role_label }} instead and reactivate it anytime.{% endif %}

{% comment %} The four outcomes are kept visibly separate on purpose: what is gone, what is kept without the user's identity, what happens to billing, and the cleanup that runs after the deletion and may be retried. See accounts/deletion.py. {% endcomment %}

Permanently deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details and any connected Google, Facebook or LinkedIn login
  • Your profile, pitch deck, videos, data room documents and the reports generated for them
  • Your connections and deal rooms, milestones, CRM leads, posts, comments, job listings and applications
  • Your notifications and settings

Kept without your identity

  • Anonymous usage statistics, such as page views and searches, no longer linked to you
  • Reports you filed about other members, and reports filed about you, so they can still be reviewed. The link to your account is removed; a report about you keeps only the username it was filed against.
  • Payment records kept by our payment provider

Finished shortly after

Your uploaded files, and your chat identity and the messages you sent, are removed right after your account is deleted. If one of those services is briefly unavailable, the removal is retried. Your account stays deleted either way. Conversations you were part of stay with the other members, without your messages.
