The Intelligence Layer

Meet Zelda.

Zelda analyzes private companies like an investment analyst, separates disclosed claims from verified evidence like a diligence associate, and connects the right counterparties like a deal team — turning raw documents into intelligence you can act on.

Information Matching Intelligence Verified Outcomes

1. Understand the Business

Founders, investors, buyers, and sellers bring their real materials into the platform — pitch decks, financials, listings — ingested securely, reducing manual data entry.

2. Find the Right Opportunities

Semantic matching connects the right businesses and capital or acquisition partners, scored by true fit, not keyword filters.

3. Analyze the Evidence

Zelda turns submitted information into intelligence. Truth Delta separates disclosed claims from verified or unsupported information — so you know what's backed by evidence before you decide.

4. Build a Verified Track Record

When a deal actually closes, both sides confirm the outcome — never a one-sided claim. Verified Funded and Verified Sold outcomes become part of the network's durable track record.
