What an evidence comparison explains

Each claim gets a status, the evidence behind it, and a reason. These invented figures and source descriptions demonstrate the format; they are not a real report or live verification.

Verified

“Our seed round raised $2 million.”

Example evidence: An investor announcement reports the same company, seed round, and USD amount.

Reason: Comparable evidence supports this specific claim.

Contradicted

“2025 revenue was $5 million.”

Example evidence: A company filing reports $3 million for the same entity, fiscal year, currency, and revenue measure.

Reason: Comparable evidence conflicts with the claimed figure.

Not established

“We have 10,000 customers.”

Example evidence: No qualifying source reports a comparable customer count.

Reason: Insufficient evidence. This does not mean the claim is false.

Actual reports include source references and limitations. Coverage depends on the company, claim, and available evidence; public filings often provide more detail than private-company sources.
