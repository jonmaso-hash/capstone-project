The Intelligence Layer

Meet Zelda.

Zelda turns authorized business materials into structured, source-linked reports and keeps missing, unavailable, contradictory, and supporting evidence distinct.

For most private companies, public sources report little, so many claims will read ‘not established’; that is a statement about the evidence, not about the company.

1. Bring in authorized materials

Pitch decks, financials, seller materials, and other documents stay tied to the user and subject that supplied them.

2. Surface alignment

Semantic similarity helps order discovery around stated profile criteria. The result is an alignment signal, not a forecast.

3. Compare claims with evidence

Truth Delta compares extracted claims against available public sources where those sources report the figure and labels each one verified, contradicted, or not established — with the reason.

4. Record confirmed outcomes

When both counterparties confirm a funding or sale outcome on Interlink, the platform records that mutual confirmation as an outcome event.

Illustrative Zelda workflow
[PROFILE] Business and counterparty criteria loaded.
[ALIGN] Stated-criteria band: NOTABLE.
[INTRO] User chooses whether to request an introduction.
[TRUTH DELTA] Claim compared with qualifying public evidence.
[REPORT] Source, status, reason, and limitations attached.
[OUTCOME] Recorded only after both parties confirm.

Illustrative only. Alignment reflects similarity between stated criteria. It is not investment advice, a prediction of transaction success, a valuation, or an assessment of company quality.
