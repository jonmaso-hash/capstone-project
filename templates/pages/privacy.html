Interlink Foundry is a private-markets marketplace. Founders, investors, business sellers and buyers use it to publish information about themselves and their companies, to be matched with counterparties, and to communicate about potential transactions. This page describes what we collect, why, and what choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Account details — username, email address, and password. Passwords are stored hashed, never in readable form.
  • Role profile — depending on whether you join as a founder, investor, seller or buyer: company name, sector or industry, stage, location, contact details, and the criteria you use to describe what you are looking for.
  • Company and deal material — pitch decks, videos, financial figures, confidential information memoranda, and any documents you upload to a data room.
  • Messages — the content of conversations you have with other participants through the platform.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic usage events — pages viewed, matches shown, introductions requested — used to operate the marketplace and improve matching.
  • Standard technical data your browser sends, such as IP address and user agent.

How your information is used

  • To create and operate your account.
  • To match you with counterparties and to explain why a match was suggested.
  • To generate the analyses and reports you request.
  • To let you communicate with other participants you have connected with.
  • To send service messages about your account, your connections, and your reports.
  • To detect and respond to abuse, fraud, and violations of our terms.

Automated processing

Some analysis on the platform is produced by automated systems, including third-party AI services used to process documents and text you submit. Automated output is informational. It is not investment, financial, legal, tax or accounting advice, and it should not be relied on as the sole basis for a transaction decision. Verify anything material independently.

Who can see what

  • Public — elevator pitches you publish to the public Explore feed are visible to anyone, including people without an account.
  • Other signed-in participants — your role profile and the information you chose to put in it.
  • Specific counterparties only — data room documents, messages, and anything shared inside a connection or deal room.
  • Nobody but you and staff — your email address and account credentials, unless you share them yourself.

You control visibility settings on your profile, including whether your profile is private and whether you accept direct messages.

Service providers

We use third parties to run the service — including payment processing, messaging and chat infrastructure, email delivery, file storage, error monitoring, and AI analysis. They receive only what they need to perform their function. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider. Card numbers are handled by that provider and are not stored on our systems.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. You can archive or delete your profile from your settings. Some records may be retained after deletion where we are required to keep them, or where they are needed to resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your profile information from your account settings.
  • Make your profile private, or turn direct messages off.
  • Unpublish an elevator pitch at any time.
  • Contact us to request deletion of your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information. Contact us and we will respond.

Security

Access to documents, data rooms and conversations is restricted to the participants involved. No system is perfectly secure; please use good judgment about what you upload, particularly before a counterparty relationship is established.

Children

Interlink Foundry is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this page changes materially, we will update the date above and notify account holders.
